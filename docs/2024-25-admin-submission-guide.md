# NUMSA 2024/25 Content Submission Guide

Use this checklist when you send in activities, stories, and updates for the website. Keep the language clear and simple. If something does not apply to your activity, mark it as “Not applicable (N/A)” instead of leaving it blank.

## 1. Contact Information
- Office or committee name
- Main contact person (full name, role)
- Email and phone number for follow-up
- Preferred publish date
- Approval status (e.g., “approved by Director of Welfare”)

## 2. Executive Offices & House Members
For profiles inside the “Leadership” sections (executives, house, junior council):
- Full name (max 50 characters)
- Current position (e.g., “Director of Socials”)
- Level (e.g., 400 Level)
- Short bio (3–4 sentences written in first person or third person)
- Portrait photo (minimum 1200px tall, JPEG or PNG)
  - File name suggestion: `position-fullname.jpg`
  - Confirm photo consent if other people appear
- Any special achievements or focus areas to highlight

## 3. Advocacy Campaigns
For awareness days and health campaigns:
- Campaign title (e.g., “World Health Day”)
- Event date or date range (write it exactly as you want it to appear)
- 3–4 sentence summary of the message or goal
- Key actions / activities carried out
- Outcomes or impact (numbers where possible)
- Call-to-action or next step (optional)
- Hero image (landscape, minimum 1600px wide)
- Up to 6 supporting photos (add captions and photographer credit)
- Quote or testimonial (optional)

## 4. Community Outreaches
For medical missions and community visits:
- Outreach title (e.g., “World Malaria Day Outreach”)
- Date and location (community name or venue)
- Host committee and supporting offices/partners
- Attendance or people served (best estimate)
- Step-by-step description (planning, setup, activities, wrap-up)
- Impact and lessons learned
- Next steps or follow-up plans
- Media package:
  - Hero photo
  - Gallery (up to 6 photos with captions)
  - Video link (optional; YouTube/Drive) with a short description
  - Confirm media consent for identifiable faces
- Credits (write-up author, photographers, partnering organizations)

## 5. Campaigns vs. Outreaches: Quick Reminder
- **Campaign** pages focus on awareness messages and online/offline advocacy.
- **Outreach** pages focus on physical activities with the community.
- If your event has both, create two submissions: one for the awareness campaign, one for the outreach execution.

## 6. Zoom Programs & Online Events
For webinars, trainings, or virtual town halls:
- Program title
- Date(s) and time(s)
- Host office
- Theme or tagline
- Speaker list (names + titles)
- Agenda overview
- Attendance numbers (or registration count)
- Key takeaways or action points
- Replay link, slide deck, or resource pack
- Event flyer or hero image (1600px wide minimum)

## 7. Blog Articles & Stories
For longer articles, reflections, or interviews:
- Working title
- Author name and office
- Publish date (or planned date)
- One-sentence summary
- Full article (300–500 words recommended)
- Optional sections: pull quotes, quick tips, resource links
- Feature image (landscape, 1600px wide)
- Additional images or graphics (optional, with captions)

## 8. Media & Files
- Place photos and videos in the shared drive folder given to your office.
- Name files clearly (e.g., `2025-jan-world-health-day-1.jpg`).
- Share raw files if possible so we can optimise for web.
- For any documents (reports, PDFs), include a short description and upload to the shared documents folder.

## 9. Submission Format
- Fill in the information in template using the fields above.
- Double-check spelling, names, and dates before sending.
- Attach media links at the bottom of the form/document.
